---
name: ambiguity-reasons
description: How to pick ambiguity_reason for a vague date request. Read it only when unsure which reason applies.
---

- unspecified_time: a day or range is clear but no exact hour ("mañana temprano", "el lunes").
- unspecified_day: a time or part of day is clear but no day ("por la tarde", "a las 10").
- ambiguous_am_pm: a bare hour that could be morning or evening ("mañana a las 2").
- other: anything else, including questions such as "¿qué días hay?".
